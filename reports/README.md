# Reports

Durable evidence about how Zygos performs. Benchmark campaigns are generated by
`eval/campaign.py report` into `benchmarks/<name>-<date>/`; this page is the
hand-maintained summary of what they showed. Append a row when a new report
directory lands.

## Results so far

The harness has not yet shown a measured improvement on a matched comparison.
Three campaigns have run, all on Terminal-Bench 2.1 at
`7131e4375048a0e408a8fb404b5f499d726b695b` with `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`:

- **Baseline is established.** The DeepSeek canary passed 11 of 24 task-runs
  for about $0.56 with zero transport or tool failures. Three tasks pass every
  time, three never pass, two are flaky.
- **Trim did not lower spend.** The only completed two-arm campaign compared
  trim on against trim off over 24 matched runs. Passes were 14 against 13,
  which is inside repeat noise. Trim-on cost 19% more and took more steps. The
  report's `candidate_better` verdict rests on scorer acceptance, not on cost.
- **The mission benchmark path works.** One task through the mission loop with
  two route tables. All-DeepSeek reached the completion gate. The arm with GLM
  as executor failed its role on step 3. One task is a smoke test, not a result.

Costs are snapshot estimates from prices frozen in each experiment file, not
invoices. Known spend across all recorded campaigns before October 8 is about
$2.08; the two October 8 campaigns add about $1.30.

the full metrics table, every matched task-run, and provenance.

## How to read a verdict

- `candidate_better` and `candidate_worse` compare the second arm against the
  first on pass delta first, then cost. Fewer than 24 matched runs is a hint,
  not a measurement, and the report says so.
- Pass deltas inside repeat noise are discounted. The report names which tasks
  flipped between repeats of the same arm.
- The trim scorer refuses any trim-on trial with zero reduction, including
  small outputs a working trimmer legitimately leaves alone. A low acceptance
  count on a trim-on arm is a scorer property, not a defect in the run.
- Mission-mode traces carry no cache counts, so mission costs are upper bounds
  and comparable only between arms of the same campaign.
